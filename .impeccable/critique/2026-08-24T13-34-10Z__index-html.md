## Overall Impression

The product is good; the screen betrays it. The biggest opportunity is inverting hierarchy: numbers that can only go down (핵심 0/3, eleven empty checkboxes, yesterday's gap) are bold and colored, while the sentences that prevent quitting are 13px gray.

## What's Working

1. The on-time window is a genuine invention: .block.open plus .badge.now plus .nowbar.active all fire together within isOnTime() ±5 minutes and expire on their own. It is a state, not a reminder, so the app cannot nag with it.
2. Emptiness is structurally impossible: AI, then templatePlan, then a catch-block templatePlan — enforced at function level rather than as an aspiration.
3. Failure copy executes the brand voice exactly: 늦게 눌러도 기록은 남아요 / 내일 계획에 자동 반영됩니다 / 지워지지 않아요. Zero adjectives, no guilt, no cheerleading.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] 60-second auto re-render eats in-flight work
boot() runs setInterval(render, 60000); render() does root.innerHTML = "" and rebuilds. Text typed into the review textarea, task input, or the four profile textareas that has not yet fired change is silently discarded up to once a minute. The settings details collapses on every write.
Why it matters: the one-line review is the only input path for the "my data feeds tomorrow's plan" differentiator, and it is the field most likely to be eaten.
Fix: stop full-document re-render; preserve details.open and activeElement/selection; debounce-save textareas on input; scope the 60s tick to .nowbar and the badges.
Command: harden

### [P0] Regeneration silently erases the day's execution record
Both .gen buttons call generatePlan(target) with no opts, so plans[targetDate] is overwritten wholesale. Every done/onTime/checkedAt for the day is lost, the counter resets to 0/N, and there is no confirm and no undo. Only replanFromNow passes keep.
Why it matters: it contradicts Principle 3 (honest records). A user who regenerates at 15:00 after a bad morning loses the two blocks they did hit on time, punishing the exact recovery behavior the app exists to encourage.
Fix: always pass keep (done plus past blocks) from every regeneration path; use a non-destructive label when a plan exists; put a full wipe behind a confirm that names the loss.
Command: harden

### [P1] The free path — the default and the portfolio demo — is broken
Verified by direct execution: templatePlan output is not chronological. 11:00-12:00 is followed by 11:00-11:10 (overlapping), and 15:00-15:10 precedes 14:00-16:00. generatePlan sorts only when opts.keep is truthy. With zero goals it yields 0 core blocks and three rows reading 딥워크 (자유).
Why it matters: key-free operation is a binding constraint, and a stranger must understand the artifact unaided. A recruiter opening the live link today sees an out-of-order timetable full of 딥워크 (자유).
Fix: sort by blockStartMinutes unconditionally at the end of generatePlan; repair the two overlaps in templatePlan; when candidates is empty, render the core slots as a first-run prompt with an inline field.
Command: harden (empty-candidate half: onboard)

### [P1] The signature badges are the least legible pixels in the app
Measured: .badge.now #fff on #f59e0b = 2.15:1; .badge.late #9aa1ab on #f1f2f4 = 2.33:1; .badge.ontime #16a34a on #e7f6ec = 2.95:1 — all at 11px. --faint (#9aa1ab) fails against every background it is used on (2.61:1 on white) and carries all times, dates, and completed-item text. The placeholder #b6bcc4 is 1.91:1. --accent on --accent-soft lands at 4.48:1, missing 4.5 by 0.02, which shows the palette was never checked. Thirty contrast failures total; --muted is the only passing token.
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md names dry eyes and adequate contrast as a real product requirement. The product's central invention is the first thing to disappear when the user is tired, which is the exact condition it was designed for.
Fix: retire --faint from text; rebuild the three badges as dark ink on light tint at 4.5:1 or better; raise the placeholder; add a dark palette, since the stated primary session is after 21:00.
Command: colorize

### [P1] The quote box and fire streak break a binding voice constraint
QUOTES ships 14 motivational lines rendered daily. renderTimeline adds a consolation sentence that is 과한 위로 verbatim. .dot.on uses --fire plus a fire emoji, which is achievement iconography.
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md marks 잔소리·응원·격려 금지 as binding and assigns failure gray, success quiet green. A reader of the brief concludes the constraint was decorative, which is worse for a portfolio than having no constraint at all.
Fix: delete .quote or replace it with the day's dry facts; recolor .dot.on to neutral ink and drop the fire emoji from the summary; cut the consolation sentence from renderTimeline.
Command: quieter

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (power user, PC, daily): every checkbox rebuilds the document, so three checks destroy focus three times and collapse an open settings panel; the 60s timer eats a half-typed review; window.prompt opens empty, so changing a key means retyping about 40 characters blind and the stored key can never be viewed; no per-block editing; no keyboard route to the primary action; .block is a label, so block text cannot be selected.

Sam (accessibility): no aria-live on the loading line, so a screen-reader user gets 30 seconds of silence after pressing the only blue button; energy chip meanings live only in the title attribute, unavailable to keyboard, touch, and screen readers, and the 28 dot dates are the same; zero aria attributes anywhere; the four profile labels are siblings with no for/id, so their accessible name is empty; the focus system is absent (one :focus rule, zero :focus-visible, outline:none on inputs, and 13 buttons plus 5 summaries plus checkboxes with no focus treatment); the core marker is a text node, so screen readers read the bullet aloud; .mini at about 29px and .dot at 13px are under 44px.

Riley (stress tester): the 21:00 cutover swaps to tomorrow while today's blocks are still unchecked and there is no date navigation, so today becomes unreachable, and templatePlan's own 21:00-22:00 row can never be checked on time; lsSet swallows every exception, so Safari private mode or a full quota loses a full day silently with no feedback; the deadline field accepts arbitrary text and forwards it to the prompt; a denied notification permission leaves a permanently dead button still labeled 켜기; cross-midnight blocks are never matched by currentBlock.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the streak grid vanished tomorrow, would this user open the app less, or more? Success is defined as returning the next day, yet the grid is the only element that renders yesterday's gap permanently.
2. The binding voice rule bans encouragement and the app ships 14 motivational quotes. Which is the real product: the dry instrument, or the thing that comforts its maker at 1am?
3. Four datasets accumulate and the UI can only ever show one day. If the differentiator is that your data feeds the next plan, why can the user never see the feedback happening?
